🥚 Egg Delivery

Careful arrangements for eggs shipped from Bantayan Island to Bacolod. Eggs need secure packaging and gentle handling along the route.

What We Accept

  • Fresh eggs in trays or cartons
  • Egg products packed for transport

Packaging Tips

  • Use sturdy egg trays and cartons
  • Reinforce outer boxes to prevent crushing
  • Confirm handling requirements with us

📦 Box Transportation

Transport arrangements for boxed goods and parcels shipped from Bantayan Island to Bacolod.

What We Accept

  • Cardboard boxes of personal goods
  • Parcels and balikbayan-style boxes
  • Small commercial shipments

Packaging Tips

  • Seal boxes securely with strong tape
  • Label boxes clearly with name and contact
  • Confirm contents are accepted before shipping

🐟 Dried Seafoods

Transport arrangements for dried seafood products from Bantayan Island. Proper packaging helps preserve product quality.

What We Accept

Packaging Tips
