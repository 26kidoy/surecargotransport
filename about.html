Company Overview

Serving Bantayan Island's Cargo Needs

SureCargo Transport is a cargo transportation and delivery service based in Bantayan Island, Cebu. We operate two primary routes — Bantayan Island to Bacolod and Bantayan Island to Cebu — and help move accepted cargo along these routes.

Our customers send a variety of goods, including eggs, boxes and parcels, dried seafoods, and commercial cargo. We work to handle each shipment with attention to the type of goods being moved.

Cargo acceptance, handling requirements, rates, and schedules are confirmed directly with the company. Please contact us before sending your cargo so we can review your needs.
